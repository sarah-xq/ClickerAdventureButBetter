# Original Plan

Originally we wanted to do an app like garage band, however the scope might have been too out of reach. After having more discussion, we decided to gamify our approach to the application. Here are some other ideas we discussed:

- Simple rhythm game app  
- Basic clicker game  
- Endless match 3 

We ultimately decided to combine the match 3 and clicker game into a brand new idea:   
A clicker incremental game that performs in levels, where each level has a unique objective, similar to level style match 3 games.

# Current Plan

Cookie Clicker came on as a conversation topic and I proposed the idea of doing an adventure themed clicker that would have levels and tasks to beat.

When it comes to the competition, we're fighting against a lot of games, however our game  
is unique in the sense that it combines every aspect of each game

\- Level and challenges of Candy Crush  
\- The grinding of clicking over and over like Cookie Clicker  
\- The automation similar to Aventure Capitalist

# Motivators and Market 

Our two main competitors for the style of application we are creating are Cookie Clicker for the incremental clicker game aspect, and Candy Crush for the level style and progression.

[https\://www\.youtube.com/watch?v=Sz4WXVNq7v8\&feature=youtu.be](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sz4WXVNq7v8&feature=youtu.be)

After being shown this video in a separate class, we noted the following about why Candy Crush is so successful:

- Level layout variance  
- Strategically placed monetization  
- Different goals make it enjoyable  
- Visible level progress


Seeing this, we wanted to add a level system mimicking the Candy Crush format, with different goals, except applied to a clicker game format.

[https\://gamificationplus.uk/cookie-clicker-gamification/](https://gamificationplus.uk/cookie-clicker-gamification/)

Here are some key takeaways from this article:

- Simple and repetitive  
- Constant positive feedback  
- Incremental style is highly rewarding

Keeping the incremental element, as well as the repetitive clicking in all of our levels will help add to the addictiveness of our application.

By keeping the simple clicking mechanic as our base, but adding variance to adventure levels helps keep the gameplay fresh, but familiar.
